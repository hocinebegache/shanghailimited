About Us

Shanghai Echo Company Limited Co, Ltd founded in 2013, is a modernized Group consisting of several companies with resources of assets, technologies, products and management. the group is involved in chemicals, food additives and machine.he founder designed and constructed the first set of SO3 sulfonation plant with each section made in China (1t/h SO3 sulfonation plant in Qiulin Chemical Manufacture Co., Nantong). We own the patent of designing and manufacturing the sulfonation reactor, which is a critical part of SO3 sulfonation plant. It uses twice protecting wind technology, which makes a further improvement in production quality, especially in the sulfonation plant with the heat-sensitive raw material. It has various advantages including having a longer continuous working period, being easier to clean the sulfonation reactor, and reducing production costs.

Apart from SO3 sulfonation plant, we also provide production plant for other surface active agents, washing powder dry molding, liquid phase, and daily chemical industry. The LAS powder plant designed and manufactured by our company can reach its purity of 80%, with its products been exported all over the world, while the vacuum thin film distillation unit used for glycerin distillation can reach its purity of at least 99.6%. ‘Sincere dedication, customers first’ is our goal, and we care about every customer’s need. According to the different requirements of customers to provide advanced technology and engineering design, can provide users with turn-key project, beginning from technical consultation, the engineering design, equipment manufacture, complete configuration, engineering installation and construction guidance, debugging, training, after-sales service until the production of qualified products, built a complete production factory.

Our company also provides customer service on the formulation technology and the technology of cosmetic products. Since the company was founded, encouraged by our success in the domestic market, we are making effort to access the international markets with high quality engineering and products, sincere service, and good reputation. To date, we offered many sulfonation plant in various countries like Peru, Philippines, Iran, Syria, Bangladesh, Tanzania, and Uzbekistan. We stand out in this competitive market and form a good partnership with customers, becoming a unique company which received unanimous praise from them.
